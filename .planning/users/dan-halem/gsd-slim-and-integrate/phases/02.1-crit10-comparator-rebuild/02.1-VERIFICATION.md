## Diagnosis

Embedding-based cosine similarity solves the **synonym/paraphrase** blindness of Jaccard (per Plan 02-07-SUMMARY-fixes.md table — "SQL injection in queryUser" ↔ "SQL injection vulnerability in queryUser" scores ~0.95 cosine vs 0.75 Jaccard). It does NOT solve the underlying problem revealed by this test: **the trimmed critics produce LARGELY DIFFERENT findings on the same input compared to the Phase 1 wave-0 baselines**, even at semantic-equivalence level.

Two non-exclusive hypotheses:

1. **Real critic-behavior drift from the trim** — Plan 02-05's bulk trim (5 critics × 263→55 lines via `@`-import to `agents/_shared/critic-base.md`) genuinely changed what each critic surfaces, not just how it phrases. The lens addendums may have lost lens-distinctive cues that the original prose carried.
2. **LLM critic nondeterminism at the finding-set level** — even N=5 median across runs doesn't reproduce a stable finding set; different runs surface genuinely different concerns within the same fixture. The 0.00 overlaps for scope/discuss suggest entire finding categories shift between runs.

Without additional evidence (a non-trimmed baseline run with N=5 against the same fixture, or H8-style variance estimate on individual critics) we can't distinguish #1 from #2. Plan 02-07's H8 was the right tool for this — and it was the scope I incorrectly dropped per MR-002 framing. (Note: the user explicitly dropped H8 as unauthorized scope; with the parity result we now know H8 would have been informational. Phase 2.1 ships as a documented finding, not a closure.)
